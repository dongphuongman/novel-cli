## Fantasy adventure style

- **Revealing the world**: no lore dumps — the world's rules emerge naturally through character interaction and action
- **Magic / power systems**: explicit costs and limits, no omnipotent abilities; show the system's edges inside conflict
- **Building epic scale**: interweave the grand narrative with a personal fate — let a small figure's point of view refract the larger picture
- **Peoples and cultures**: different peoples and cultures have distinct speech habits, values and patterns of behavior
- **Combat**: turns on strategy and cost rather than raw power crushing the opposition; win through terrain and wit
- **Journey narrative**: every new setting has its own visual and sensory signature — never "another village"
- **Growth arc**: the protagonist's growth in capability keeps pace with their growth in judgment; every gain has its price
- **Naming**: keep the register consistent; an incongruous modern word breaks immersion
