## Romance style

- **Emotional progression**: follow the natural rhythm of contact → attraction → conflict → reconciliation → deepening; do not rush it
- **Relational tension**: every stage needs a credible obstacle, and the obstacles should vary in source (temperament, position, misunderstanding, outside pressure)
- **Interiority**: go deep into a character's inner life and show the contradiction and the struggle — but avoid long soliloquy-style psychological analysis
- **Detail in interaction**: micro-expressions, small gestures and unconscious habits carry more charge than a stated declaration
- **Dialogue rhythm**: much left unsaid during the ambiguous phase, light and easy once things are sweet, sharp but restrained during conflict
- **Atmosphere**: let the setting echo the emotional state, without overusing stock metaphors of the "rain = sadness" kind
- **Function of the supporting cast**: the best-friend or sibling figure drives the plot forward rather than serving as a romance-advice dispenser
- **Conflict design**: a misunderstanding that "one sentence would clear up" cannot sustain a conflict; the friction has to reach a real difference in values
